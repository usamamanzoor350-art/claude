# BirthdayUniverse.co — CEO Roadmap (Grok system)

Owner ki nazar se: Grok humari **market ki aankhein** hai. Wo research karta hai, angles laata hai aur hamara kaam draft karta hai.
Faisle aap karti ho. Store, designs aur ads ka kaam Claude karta hai.

---

## 1. Sab se bunyadi baat: Grok ko "sahi track" par kaise rakhna hai

Grok khud se seekh nahi leta. Wo **utna hi acha** kaam karta hai jitni **sachi aur taza** maloomat hum usay dete hain.
Isliye 4 "sach ki files" banao aur har project mein upload rakho. Ye humari company ki yaaddasht hain.

| File | Kya hai | Kab update |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Master Brief** | Brand, products, goal, voice (bana hua hai) | Har mahine |
| **2. Customer Truth** | Gen X customer ke asal alfaz, dard, khushi, nostalgia triggers (Grok ki research se) | Har 2 hafte |
| **3. Winners & Losers Log** | Har ad ka result: kya chala, kya fail, kyun | **Har hafte** (sab se zaroori) |
| **4. Ad Bank** | Saare tested aur untested angles, har design ke liye | Har hafte |

**Rule:** Grok ki koi baat tab tak in files mein nahi jayegi jab tak:
- uska **link/proof** na ho, ya
- **humare ads ne usay sach sabit** na kiya ho.

Isi tarah Grok ghalat track par nahi jayega.

### Winners & Losers Log ka format (Google Doc / Sheet)
```
Date | Ad name | Design | Angle | Gift/Self | Spend | CTR | CPC | Add to cart | Purchases | ROAS | Faisla (scale/kill/iterate) | Kyun (1 line)
```

## 3. Automations: jab aap so rahi ho tab bhi kaam chale

Grok ke **Automations** mein ye 6 banao. Har ek ke prompt ke shuru mein ye likho: "Master Brief aur Customer Truth file parho."

**A. Roz subah 7 baje: Trend Scout**
```
Pichhle 24 ghante mein US mein X, TikTok, Instagram aur Reddit par birthday, milestone birthday
(40/50/60), aur Gen X nostalgia se jude top 5 trends/memes/phrases. Har ek: kya hai, link,
aur humari kis shirt ke liye kaunsa static ad ban sakta hai (headline + image idea).
```

**B. Roz raat 9 baje: Comment Miner (Gen X ki asal awaaz)**
```
Aaj r/GenX, r/AskWomenOver40, Facebook/IG/TikTok birthday posts ke comments, aur X par
Gen X women (45-60) ne apni birthday, umar, aur 70s/80s/90s yaadon ke baare mein jo kaha —
10 asal quotes (link ke saath). Har quote ke saath: ye kis ad headline mein badal sakta hai.
```

**C. Har Somvaar: Ad Library Spy**
```
Meta Ad Library (US): birthday shirt, milestone birthday gift, Gen X nostalgia ke wo static
ads jo 30+ din se active hain. Top 10: headline, visual, offer, link. Humare 5 designs ke liye
5 naye angles jo Ad Bank mein nahi hain.
```

**D. Har Budh: Competitor Watch**
```
Humare top 10 competitors (Etsy, Amazon, Shopify birthday tee/gift sellers) ne is hafte kya
naya kiya: naye designs, price change, offers, bundles, ads. Sirf jo badla hai wo batao.
```

**E. Har Jumma: Social Content Plan (Instagram + TikTok)**
```
Agle hafte ke liye Instagram aur TikTok ka content plan: 5 posts + 5 stories.
Har post: format (carousel/static/reel idea), hook, caption (US English), hashtags (10),
kaunsi shirt, aur kis din/time post karna hai (US Eastern). Gen X nostalgia + humor + gifting mix.
```

**F. Har mahine ki 1 tareekh: Occasion Planner**
```
Agle 90 din ke US birthday/gifting moments, aur har ek ke liye kab ads shuru karne hain.
Table: Date | Moment | Humari shirt/bundle | Ad kab shuru.
```

> **Sach:** Grok Instagram ya TikTok par **khud post nahi kar sakta**. Wo content **likh kar deta hai**.
> Posting ke liye **Meta Business Suite** (Instagram + Facebook schedule) aur **TikTok ka scheduler** use karo.
> Har Jumma Grok ka plan aata hai, aur 30 minute mein poore hafte ki posts schedule ho jati hain.

## 4. Asal masla: Gen X ko kharidne par kaise laana hai

Conversion ke liye Grok se ye 5 cheezein research karwao (01 Research Director, ek ek chat).
Har jawab **Customer Truth** file mein jayega.

1. **Wo kyun kharidti hain?** Khud ke liye vs gift. Kaunsa jazba hai: hasi, fakhr, nostalgia, "I made it".
2. **Wo kyun nahi kharidti?** Fit ka dar, quality ka shak, "ye mere liye bahut young hai", price, shipping time. Har dar ka jawab product page par hona chahiye.
3. **Unhein kya bharosa dilata hai?** Reviews, real (umar wali) models, size guide, return policy, "Made in USA" ya cotton quality.
4. **Wo kahan hain?** Facebook vs Instagram vs Pinterest. Gen X Facebook par zyada hai, isliye check karwao.
5. **Kaunse alfaz?** Unki zabaan mein headlines: "made it", "key kid", "still no curfew", "zero f's".

**Prompt (01 Research Director):**
```
Master Brief aur Customer Truth parho. US Gen X women (45-60) online birthday apparel kyun
NAHI kharidti — top 10 wajahen (reviews, Reddit, comments se, links ke saath). Har wajah ke liye:
product page par kya likhein/dikhayein, aur kaunsa ad angle ye shak door karega.
```

## 6. 90 din ka roadmap

**Din 1–30: Bunyad + pehle winners**
- 4 sach ki files tayyar (Customer Truth ki pehli research).
- Har hafte 5 static ads test ($15/din har ek).
- Goal: **2–3 winning angles** (ROAS 2.5x+) aur average order $50+ (bundle).

**Din 31–60: Winners ko barhao**
- Winner angles ke 3–3 naye versions (naya scene, naya rang, nayi age).
- Winner ads ka budget har 3 din 20% badhao.
- Ab video ki taraf: winning static angle ko UGC video mein badlo.
- Instagram/TikTok organic rozana.

**Din 61–90: Scale + nayi products**
- Naye products/bundles jo research ne sabit kiye (squad shirts, gift box, jewelry).
- Email/SMS flows (abandoned cart, birthday reminder).
- Har mahine Master Brief + Customer Truth refresh.

## 7. Kya kabhi nahi karna
- Grok ki bina link wali baat ko sach maan lena.
- Ek ad ko 2–3 hafte se zyada bina naye version ke chalana.
- Ek hi dafa 20 cheezein shuru karna. Har hafte sirf TOP 3.
- Winners & Losers Log update na karna. Iske bina Grok kabhi seekh nahi payega.
