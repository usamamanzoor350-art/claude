OLD WINNERS — Birthday Clothing (purana brand) ke winning mockups ka analysis (25 mockups)
Ye PROVEN visual formulas hain — naye ads inhi par banao, phir naye angles test karo.

1. DESIGNS JO JEETE
- "I Am [Age-1] +" middle finger (red nail) — 39+, 57+, 59+ → humara Finger design.
- "Vintage [Year]" retro — 1962-1969 → humara Vintage design.
- "Hello Sixty, Est. 1966" + "Sixty-licious" script → humara Hello design.

2. UMAR
- Zyada tar 57-64 (born 1962-1969) — yaani 60th ke aas paas.
  Naye store ka best seller bhi Hello 65 hai → 60-65 hamara sweet spot.

3. RANG
- Black aur Navy sab se zyada (dark shirt + rang-birangi/white print).
- Finger ke liye washed colours bhi chale (pepper grey, blue jean, rust/orange).

4. PHOTO KA ANDAZ
- Chehra aksar kata hua ya aadha — focus SHIRT par (chest se ghutnon tak).
- Real jagahen: lakdi ki deewar, kitchen, brick wall, street, Vegas strip, desert, wine bar.
- Poses: dono haathon se shirt ki taraf ishara (pointing), cross-legged jeans mein baith-na,
  wine glass, cowboy hat, leopard skirt (girls-trip / party vibe), do saheliyan saath (matching).
- Natural grey / silver hair wali real women (Vintage ke liye).
- Image par koi headline/text nahi — sirf shirt. Simple aur saaf.

5. NAYE ADS KE LIYE RULES
- Pehle inhi formulas ki copy (naye store ke designs ke saath): black/navy, pointing pose,
  real location, chehra crop, 60-65 age.
- Phir ek ek cheez badal kar test: (a) headline add karo (Grok ke Gen X angles), (b) naya scene,
  (c) gift angle (beti/saheli).
- Girls-trip / Vegas / wine wala lifestyle 60th ke liye khaas taur par try karo.
