# Refund + Shipping policy (Shopify: Settings → Policies → paste)

## Refund policy

At Birthday Universe, every piece is customized with love and care, just for you. We truly want you to love your order, and if something isn't quite right, we're here to help.

**30-Day Returns**
You have 30 days from the day your order is delivered to request a return. To be eligible, the item should be unworn, unwashed and in the same condition you received it, together with your order number or proof of purchase.

To start a return, simply email us at help@birthdayuniverse.co with your order number. We'll reply with friendly instructions and a return address. Please contact us before sending anything back, as packages sent without a return request can't be processed.

**Damaged, Defective or Wrong Item**
Please check your order when it arrives. If anything is damaged, misprinted, defective or not what you ordered, email us within 30 days with your order number and a photo. We'll gladly send a free replacement or a full refund. You won't need to return the item.

**Exchanges**
Need a different size or color? Just let us know. The quickest way is to return the original item and place a new order for the one you'd like.

**Refunds**
Once we receive and check your return, we'll email you to confirm. Approved refunds go back to your original payment method within 5–10 business days. Please note that original shipping costs are non-refundable.

## Shipping policy

Thank you for choosing Birthday Universe! Every order is customized with love and care, just for you, so it is made to order rather than pulled from a shelf.

**Delivery Time**
Orders are delivered within 10–15 business days. This includes the time we take to carefully customize, print and check your order, plus shipping to your door.

Planning a birthday surprise? We recommend ordering a little early so your gift arrives in time for the big day.

**Shipping Cost**
Enjoy free shipping on orders over $75. For smaller orders, the shipping cost is shown at checkout before you pay.

**Order Tracking**
As soon as your order ships, we'll email you a tracking link so you can follow it all the way to your door.

**Sending a Gift?**
Simply enter the recipient's address at checkout. We never include prices or invoices in the package, so it's ready to gift.

**Delays**
Once in a while, carriers may be delayed by weather, holidays or high volume. If your order is taking longer than expected, please reach out and we'll happily check on it for you.

**Address Accuracy**
Please double-check your shipping address at checkout. If you notice a mistake, email us as soon as possible and we'll do our best to update it before your order ships.
