Blush & Burgundy

One color system for the website, the Shopify store, product images, ads and videos. Every design uses only these six colors, plus the shirts themselves.

The six colors

How much of each

60% White + Blush
30% Burgundy
10%

Mostly calm and light, a strong burgundy for the brand, and a small touch of gold to make it feel like a party. Rose is only for soft extras (hover states, thin lines, small details).

Where each color goes

Website & store

  • Page background: Warm White
  • Some sections (hero, reviews): Blush
  • Logo, headings, buttons, announcement bar, footer: Burgundy
  • Body text: Ink
  • "Best Seller" badges, small stars: Gold

Product images

  • Background: Warm White (or Blush for dark shirts)
  • Header bars and pills: Burgundy with white text
  • Confetti, thin icons: Gold
  • Tag "BIRTHDAY FAVORITE": Blush with Burgundy text

Ads, videos, social

  • Same palette as the product images
  • Text on video: white on a Burgundy band
  • End card: Burgundy background, white logo, gold confetti

Which background for which shirt

Light shirts disappear on pink. Always check the shirt stands out.

Do and don't

✓ Do

  • Keep lots of white space; let the shirts be the most colorful thing
  • Use Burgundy for every "Add to cart" and main button
  • Use Gold in small amounts: confetti, badges, stars
  • Put light shirts on Warm White

✗ Don't

  • Make whole pages pink
  • Add new colors (teal, purple, neon, rainbow)
  • Write text in Gold or Rose on white (too hard to read)
  • Put the Light Pink shirt on a Blush background
