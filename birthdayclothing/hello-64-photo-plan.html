3 · What the research says

People want to see it worn

Model photos help buyers picture the shirt on themselves, judge size and fit, and see it on different body types.

Lifestyle beats a floating shirt

One Etsy seller guide says floating shirts on white convert about 40% worse than lifestyle shots. Treat that number as a seller's claim, not a study, but the direction is consistent across guides.

A small repeatable stack wins

Guides recommend a set you can repeat for every product: a clean flat lay, a lifestyle angle, a close crop and a hero image. Clarity on a phone matters more than fancy effects.

Show real fabric

Good mockups show realistic folds, shadows and light. A pasted-on print looks fake and lowers trust.
