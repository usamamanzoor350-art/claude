# birthdayclothing.com — Omni Flash video ad prompt
## "The Birthday Tee Tree" (princess + prince + elephant, fairy-tale format)

Format reference: the viral 3D-animated short where a duckling can't reach a cherry,
something snatches it, then a friendly elephant shakes the tree and cherries rain down.
Same beat structure here — but the cherries are **birthdayclothing.com tees**, and the
characters are an original fairy-tale princess, her prince, a witch and a royal elephant.

> Note: characters are *Cinderella-inspired*, not Disney's Cinderella. Using Disney's exact
> character designs in a paid ad is a copyright/trademark risk, so the prompt describes an
> original princess in a similar storybook style.

---

## How to use in Omni Flash

1. **Attach all product images as reference images** (upload each one):
   - `REF 1` — pink tee, "hello FIFTY ♥ EST. 1976" (black print)
   - `REF 2` — charcoal tee, "The Queen is 79" (white script, gold crown, gold fireworks)
   - `REF 3`, `REF 4` … — any more tees you add (hello THIRTY, hello SIXTY, etc.)
2. Paste the **MASTER PROMPT** below.
3. If Omni Flash only makes short clips (~8 s), use the **3-CLIP VERSION** instead and join
   the clips in CapCut. Use the last frame of each clip as the start image of the next so the
   characters stay the same.
4. AI video often garbles printed text. If the tee text comes out wrong, keep the best clip
   and put a sharp product-photo overlay / text in CapCut for the close-up shots.

## MASTER PROMPT (copy everything in the box)

```
Create a vertical 9:16, 25-second, premium 3D animated advertisement in a Pixar-style
storybook look: soft cinematic lighting, warm golden-hour sun, shallow depth of field,
fluffy detailed textures, expressive big-eyed characters, smooth camera moves, high detail,
4K quality. No dialogue, only gentle magical orchestral music and cute sound effects.

PRODUCTS (MUST MATCH REFERENCE IMAGES EXACTLY):
Use the attached reference images as the exact T-shirts in this video. Keep every shirt's
color, print, font, layout and text identical to the references, with the text clearly
readable and correctly spelled:
- REF 1: soft pink crew-neck tee, black print "hello" in script, "FIFTY" in bold capitals
  with a small heart, and "EST. 1976" below.
- REF 2: charcoal grey crew-neck tee, white script "The Queen is 79" with a shiny gold crown
  on the "T" and gold fireworks behind "79".
- Any other attached reference tees: reproduce them exactly as shown.
Do not invent new designs, do not change the text, do not add extra logos.

CHARACTERS (original, storybook style):
- THE PRINCESS: a kind, graceful young princess with a soft blonde updo, sparkling blue eyes,
  a flowing pale-blue ball gown and a small tiara. Very cute and expressive.
- THE PRINCE: a warm, charming prince with brown hair, a navy-and-gold royal jacket and a
  gentle smile.
- THE WITCH: a funny, sneaky (not scary) old witch with a crooked purple hat, green cloak and
  a long pointy nose. Comedic, cartoon villain energy, family-friendly.
- THE ELEPHANT: a big, adorable baby-blue-grey royal elephant with huge soft eyes and pink
  ears, wearing a small golden head ornament. Friendly and loyal to the prince.

SETTING:
A magical fairy-tale castle garden beside a sparkling turquoise lake, a fairy-tale castle
softly blurred in the background, green hills, flowers and floating sparkles. In the center
stands a big magical tree. Instead of fruit, the tree's branches are hanging with neatly
folded and hanging birthday T-shirts from the references, swaying gently like fruit on
branches, glowing faintly with magical light.

STORY / SHOTS:
0–3 s: Close-up low angle. One perfect pink "hello FIFTY" tee (REF 1) hangs from a single
branch just above the princess. She stands on tiptoe, reaching up with both arms, eyes
shining with hope. The tee is clearly visible and readable.

3–6 s: Suddenly the witch swoops in on her broomstick with a cheeky cackle, snatches the
"hello FIFTY" tee off the branch and zooms away. The princess freezes with a shocked,
open-mouth surprised face, then her lips tremble, sad eyes, tiny tear.

6–9 s: Wide shot. The prince walks up with his giant cute royal elephant. The elephant's
huge body fills half the frame as it gently steps in next to the tiny sad princess. The
prince looks at her with love, then looks up at the tree full of shirts.

9–13 s: Close-up of the elephant looking up at the tree, then wrapping its trunk around the
main branch loaded with birthday tees (REF 1, REF 2 and the other reference designs). The
princess looks up, curious. The prince smiles and nods.

13–17 s: The elephant shakes the whole tree playfully. Slow motion: dozens of birthday
T-shirts rain down from the tree like confetti — pink "hello FIFTY" tees, charcoal
"The Queen is 79" tees and the other designs, fluttering and spinning in the golden light
with sparkles. Some land softly on the grass, some on the princess.

17–21 s: The princess bursts into a huge, joyful laugh, eyes closed with happiness, catching
tees and hugging a "hello FIFTY" tee to her chest. Quick magic sparkle transition: she is
now WEARING the pink "hello FIFTY" tee (print clearly visible on her chest, front facing
camera) over her gown skirt, twirling happily.

21–24 s: Heartwarming close-up: the princess, wearing the tee, hugs the elephant's trunk,
and the prince joins with a smile. The elephant closes its eyes happily. In the background
the witch peeks from behind a bush, now wearing "The Queen is 79" tee, looking pleased.

24–25 s: End card: soft dim magical tree silhouette with fallen tees on the grass, big clean
white text in the center: "birthdayclothing.com" and below it "Every birthday deserves a
fairy tale." Gentle sparkle, music ends.

STYLE RULES:
Consistent characters in every shot, consistent T-shirt designs in every shot, vertical
9:16 framing with subjects centered, smooth natural motion, no morphing faces, no extra
limbs, no watermarks, no Instagram or social media logos, no random text other than the
T-shirt prints and the final end-card text.
```

## Quick variations (same prompt, swap the hero tee)

- **Mom's 50th**: hero tee = REF 1 "hello FIFTY" (as written above).
- **Grandma's birthday**: make the hero tee REF 2 "The Queen is 79" and let the princess
  become a sweet grandma queen with a crown; the witch still steals it, elephant still saves it.
- **Hello THIRTY / SIXTY**: attach that tee as REF 1 and replace "hello FIFTY" in the text.
