How the colors match everywhere

  • Website: warm white background, burgundy for the logo, text, buttons and the announcement bar, blush for some sections, gold only for small highlights.
  • Product images: the same warm white or blush backgrounds, burgundy header bars and pills, gold confetti. Light shirts (pink, white, sand) go on warm white; dark shirts (charcoal, navy, black) can go on blush.
  • Ads and videos: same palette, so an ad, the website and the product photos look like one brand.
  • Rule: 60% warm white and blush, 30% burgundy, 10% gold. No other colors, except the shirts themselves.
