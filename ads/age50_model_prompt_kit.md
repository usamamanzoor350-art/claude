# Age 50 — Model + Product Image Prompt Kit (ChatGPT)

## STEP 1 — Model (sirf ek baar banao, phir har image me yahi upload karo)

```
Ultra-realistic studio photograph of a real 50-year-old white American woman, the kind of woman you'd see at a Target in Ohio or a school pickup line — NOT a model, NOT an influencer.

AGE MUST READ CLEARLY AS 50 (not 35, not 40):
- visible crow's feet and laugh lines, soft nasolabial folds, slight under-eye puffiness
- softer jawline, faint neck lines, a little loose skin under the chin
- real skin texture: visible pores, a few sun spots/freckles on cheeks and chest, slight redness, no smoothing, no retouching
- shoulder-length dark-blonde hair with natural grey strands and 1 cm of grey at the roots, slightly frizzy, not salon-styled
- light everyday makeup only (a bit of mascara, tinted lip balm)
- body: realistic US size 12–14, soft midsection, fuller upper arms, natural posture — not slim, not toned
- warm, friendly, slightly tired-but-happy smile, teeth natural (not bright white)

Wearing a plain heather-grey cotton crew-neck t-shirt and mid-wash straight-leg jeans, barefoot or plain white sneakers. Standing straight, arms relaxed, full body visible head to shoes.

Background: plain seamless light warm-grey paper backdrop (#E6E4E0), soft natural shadow on the floor behind her, nothing else in frame.

Camera: shot on Canon EOS R6, 50mm lens, f/5.6, one large softbox from front-left plus fill, true-to-life colors, slight film grain. Looks like a real e-commerce catalog photo, not AI, not CGI, not plastic skin, no perfect symmetry.

Vertical 4:5 (1080x1350). Keep her whole body inside the frame with ~8% empty space above the head and below the feet so nothing gets cut on Facebook/Instagram feed, stories or Shopify.
```

Agar phir bhi young lage, ye line add karo:
`Make her look 3–4 years OLDER than you think 50 looks. She should look like a grandmother of a toddler.`

## STEP 2 — Har shirt image (template)

Har baar upload karo: (1) Step 1 wali model image, (2) us design ki asli design file/mockup.

```
Use the uploaded woman as the EXACT same person: same face, same age, same hair, same body size. Do not make her younger or slimmer.

She is now wearing a [COLOR] [ROUND NECK / V-NECK] 100% cotton t-shirt with the uploaded design printed on the chest exactly as provided — same text, same spelling, same font, same layout, centered, about 10 inches wide. Print looks like real DTG/screen print: slight ink texture, follows the fabric folds, not floating on top.

Fit: unisex regular fit, true to size (she's wearing size L). Hem sits just below the hip, sleeves end mid-upper-arm. Natural cotton wrinkles at the waist and elbows, soft fabric drape — it must look like a real cotton tee, not a smooth rendered fabric.

Bottoms: mid-wash straight-leg jeans. No jewelry except small stud earrings.

Pose: [POSE TEXT]

Background: plain seamless light warm-grey backdrop (#E6E4E0), soft floor shadow, nothing else in frame. Focus is the shirt.

Camera: Canon EOS R6, 50mm, f/5.6, softbox lighting, true-to-life color of the shirt, slight film grain, real catalog photo. No AI look, no plastic skin, no extra fingers, no warped text.

Vertical 4:5 (1080x1350). Whole subject inside frame with ~8% space top and bottom so it displays fully on FB/IG feed, stories and Shopify.
```

## STEP 3 — Flat lay (7th image)

ChatGPT text galat likhta hai, isliye flat lay **bina text** banao — text main (Claude) clean overlay kar dunga.

```
Overhead flat-lay product photo of three folded 100% cotton t-shirts arranged neatly on a plain warm off-white surface (#F3F0EB): one [COLOR A] Round Neck in front showing the uploaded design fully on the chest, behind it partially visible a [COLOR B] V-Neck and a [COLOR C] Round Neck, slightly fanned. Real cotton texture, soft natural wrinkles, visible stitching on the collar and sleeve hem, small woven neck label.

Leave the top 25% and bottom 20% of the image completely empty (plain surface) for text to be added later. Do NOT add any text, letters or logos except the shirt design.

Soft daylight from the top-left, gentle realistic shadows, shot from directly above on a Canon EOS R6, 35mm, true-to-life colors. Real e-commerce photo, not AI-looking.

Vertical 4:5 (1080x1350).
```
